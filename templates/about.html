Step 1 of 4 · What is AIMIS

Practice job interviews before the real one

AIMIS (AI Mock Interview System) lets you practice job interviews with an AI interviewer. It talks with you like a real interviewer for the job you want, then shows you what went well and what to improve.

  • {{ ui_icon("briefcase", 22) }}Set in your target jobQuestions fit the job you're preparing for, like IT, BPO or teaching.
  • {{ ui_icon("chat", 22) }}Focused on communication and behaviorIt checks how you answer and how you come across, not technical knowledge.
  • {{ ui_icon("history", 22) }}Practice as often as you wantEvery interview is saved so you can see your progress.

Step 2 of 4 · How an interview works

A real conversation, not a quiz

  1. Pick a skill and a levelFor example Teamwork, Beginner, 4 questions.
  2. AIMIS Bot asks out loudIn your first interview it starts by asking your name and about yourself.
  3. You answer by speakingYour microphone records your answer and your camera checks eye contact, expression and gestures.
  4. It replies and follows upIf your answer has a real example, it asks a follow-up about it. If not, it moves on to the next question.

An interview takes about 5 to 10 minutes. Use a quiet room with good lighting.

Step 3 of 4 · How you're scored

Two scores, one readiness result

60% Answer Effectiveness

What you said: relevance, directness, structure (STAR) and specific examples.

40% Communication Delivery

How you said it: fluency, clarity, vocal confidence and grammar, plus eye contact, facial expression and gestures.

Together they make your Interview Readiness score. 70 or higher meets the standard.
