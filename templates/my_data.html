Where your data goes

  • Camera: analyzed only in your browser. Video is never recorded or uploaded; only scores such as eye contact are saved.
  • Voice: each answer is recorded and stored encrypted on the AIMIS server. It is sent over a secure connection to Google Gemini, a third-party AI service, to turn your speech into text and score your answers. Google's free service may use this content to improve its products.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically after {{ days }} days. Your transcripts, scores and feedback stay until you delete them below.
  • AIMIS Coach: runs on the AIMIS computer with a local AI (Ollama). If it isn't available, Gemini may be used as a backup.
  • Evaluators (teachers or HR practitioners helping check the AI's accuracy) may read the text of your answers without your name, recording or scores.
  • Administrators can see your name, scores and feedback, but not your recordings, transcripts or chats.
  • Your password is stored as a one-way hash; nobody can read it.

Delete my interviews

Deletes all your interviews, recordings, transcripts, scores, feedback and chatbot history. Your account stays, so you can keep practicing. This can't be undone.

Delete my account

Deletes your account and everything above. You will be logged out and can't log in again with this email unless you register again. This can't be undone.
