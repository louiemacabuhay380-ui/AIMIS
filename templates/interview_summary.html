What does each score measure?
  • Response Quality: the AI rates each answer's relevance, clarity, completeness (situation, action, result), and grammar from 0 to 100, then averages them.
  • Speech Performance: the average of fluency, speech clarity, and confidence. Confidence is measured from your pauses, tone, volume steadiness, and filler words.
  • Behavioral: the average of eye contact, facial expression, and gestures, measured by your camera on your own device. Gestures score higher when you use open-hand gestures while speaking, and lower when you touch your face or move your hands very fast.
  • Overall: each score is multiplied by how much it counts (60%, 25%, and 15%), then added together. The percentages next to each bar are these weights, not your scores.
